<h2>Purpose</h2>

<p>
  Present a self-contained state of the art on agricultural mobile robotics,
  human-aware navigation, proxemics, motion planning, local control, perception,
  simulation, and evaluation practices for shared agricultural environments.
</p>

<h2>Coverage</h2>

<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Area</th><th>Topics</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Agricultural robotics</td><td>Structured crops, vineyard and orchard corridors, row following, localization, and shared mobility.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Human-aware navigation</td><td>Social conventions, legibility, predictability, comfort, and interaction-aware motion.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Proxemics</td><td>Interpersonal space, proxemic regions, orientation, anisotropy, relative motion, and groups.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Planning architecture</td><td>Global planning, local control, hierarchical navigation, and modular robotic systems.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Social avoidance</td><td>Dynamic Window Approach, TEB, ORCA/RVO, Social Force Model, anisotropic fields, and control barrier functions.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Perception</td><td>LiDAR, vision, localization, human detection, and sensor fusion.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Simulation and evaluation</td><td>Instrumented environments, telemetry, safety, comfort, efficiency, naturalness, and legibility.</td></tr>
  </tbody>

<h2>Role in the documentation set</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Introduces the terminology used in agricultural social navigation.</li>
  <li>Organizes the main planning and interaction-aware control families.</li>
  <li>Summarizes perception, simulation, and evaluation practices reported in the literature.</li>
  <li>Provides the same thematic structure in English and Spanish.</li>
  <li>Consolidates technical references by research topic.</li>
</ul>
